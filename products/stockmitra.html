Proprietary Software Product

StockMitra
Smart Inventory & POS Billing Platform

Engineered specifically for retailers, wholesalers, distributors, and multi-branch merchants. Eliminate stock discrepancies, speed up customer checkout counters, generate GST tax invoices instantly, and gain 100% control over your business inventory.

Schedule Live StockMitra Demo Chat with Product Team
Product Capabilities

Built for Real-World Retail & Warehouse Demands

Designed with clean touch-friendly screens and keyboard shortcuts for rapid counter operations.

Fast Billing

Lightning Barcode POS Checkout

Scan items using USB barcode scanners or mobile cameras. Support for quick item search, batch pricing, thermal receipt printing, and multiple payment splits.

  • Compatible with 2-inch and 3-inch thermal printers
  • Split tender (Cash + UPI + Card)
  • Keyboard-only rapid checkout mode
Stock Control

Real-Time Multi-Branch Inventory

Monitor exact stock levels across multiple stores and storage godowns in real-time. Transfer stock between branches with digital challans and audit verification.

  • Low-stock automated threshold alerts
  • Inter-branch stock transfer ledger
  • Physical stock audit reconciliation tools
Tax & Compliance

Automated GST Invoicing

Generate compliant GST tax invoices (B2B & B2C) with auto CGST/SGST/IGST breakdown, HSN/SAC codes, and instant PDF sharing directly to customer WhatsApp.

  • GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B ready Excel summaries
  • E-way bill & delivery challan generation
  • Customizable store logo and terms printout
Procurement

Supplier & Purchase Order System

Track supplier rate lists, generate official purchase orders, record Goods Receipt Notes (GRN), and maintain accurate vendor payment outstanding balances.

  • Purchase order to GRN 1-click conversion
  • Supplier ledger with debit note support
  • Purchase cost vs selling price margin analytics
Customer Accounts

Khata & Credit Management

Maintain digital credit ledgers (Udhar Khata) for loyal customers. Send automated payment reminder notifications via WhatsApp and accept UPI settlements.

  • WhatsApp payment link dispatch
  • Customer purchase history & credit limits
  • Loyalty discount points on billing
Business Intelligence

Owner Mobile Dashboard

Access your business numbers from anywhere. Monitor real-time daily revenue, profit margins, top-selling products, and dead stock from your smartphone.

  • Live sales count & margin calculation
  • Cash register opening and closing tally
  • End-of-day summary reports via email
Reliability Engineering

Built for Continuous Uptime & Data Security

Zero data loss architecture designed to withstand power cuts and internet dropouts.

Offline-First Resilience

Continue scanning and printing bills even if your internet connection goes down. Data syncs automatically once reconnected.

Role-Based Access

Restrict billing staff from viewing sensitive wholesale purchase costs or deleting past invoices without manager approval.

Automated Cloud Backup

Your transactions and stock ledgers are securely backed up to encrypted cloud storage every night with instant restore capability.
