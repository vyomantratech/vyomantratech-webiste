About the Role

Vyomantra Technologies is creating intelligent systems that transform how companies operate. From autonomous customer service agents and multi-modal document intelligence to predictive inventory models for StockMitra, our AI engineers tackle real enterprise challenges.

As an AI & Machine Learning Engineer, you will design, fine-tune, evaluate, and deploy neural network architectures, vector search databases, and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) microservices. You will work directly with modern LLMs, embeddings, and computer vision pipelines to ship real-world products.

Responsibilities

  • Architect and deploy scalable AI microservices with Python, FastAPI, and Docker.
  • Build resilient RAG pipelines using vector stores (Pinecone, Chroma, pgvector) and embedding models.
  • Fine-tune open-weight models (Llama, Mistral, Gemma) using LoRA / QLoRA for domain-specific automation.
  • Develop computer vision and OCR pipelines for automated invoice, receipt, and identity verification.
  • Implement model monitoring, drift detection, prompt evaluation frameworks, and latency optimization.

Eligibility & Requirements

Qualifications & Background:

B.Tech/B.E./M.Tech/M.S. in Computer Science, Data Science, AI, or equivalent practical industry experience (1–3+ years). Fresh graduates with standout Kaggle rankings or production AI open-source contributions are strongly welcomed.

Technical Prerequisites:

  • Deep fluency in Python and data science stacks (NumPy, Pandas, PyTorch, or TensorFlow).
  • Hands-on experience with LLM orchestration frameworks (LangChain, LlamaIndex, or AutoGen).
  • Experience building and deploying RESTful APIs via FastAPI and containerizing via Docker.
  • Understanding of vector databases, similarity metrics (cosine, dot product), and prompt engineering.
