About the Role

Vyomantra Technologies is in an aggressive growth phase. Beyond engineering top-tier digital products, we enable enterprise brands and regional powerhouses to dominate search results and acquire qualified buyers through precision digital marketing.

As our Digital Marketing & Growth Specialist, you will oversee end-to-end inbound marketing strategies. You will manage paid ad budgets, execute on-page and technical SEO, write compelling copy, build conversion funnels, and analyze multi-touch attribution metrics to maximize return on ad spend (ROAS).

Responsibilities

  • Plan, execute, and optimize paid campaigns across Google Search/Display, Meta (Facebook & Instagram), and LinkedIn.
  • Conduct technical SEO audits, keyword research, backlink acquisition, and metadata optimization to drive organic rankings.
  • Create conversion-focused landing page copy, ad creatives, email outreach sequences, and case studies.
  • Track multi-channel funnels via Google Analytics 4 (GA4), Google Tag Manager (GTM), and client CRM dashboards.
  • Continuously run A/B split tests on ad hooks, headlines, CTAs, and landing pages to boost conversion rates.

Eligibility & Requirements

Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, Computer Applications, or equivalent proven digital marketing track record (1–2+ years experience managing live ad budgets or high-traffic SEO properties).

Required Skills:

  • Proven experience with Meta Ads Manager and Google Ads (Search, Performance Max, Display).
  • Hands-on familiarity with SEO platforms (SEMrush, Ahrefs, Google Search Console, Screaming Frog).
  • Deep understanding of conversion rate optimization (CRO) and user acquisition funnel metrics (CPA, CAC, ROAS).
  • Exceptional written and verbal communication skills; ability to articulate technical value propositions clearly.
