The Business Challenge

Unstructured DMs & Lost Orders During Festival Rush

Home Bakers previously took customized orders manually through Instagram direct messages and telephone calls. During festive holidays like Diwali and Christmas, their small team became overwhelmed with over 150+ unstructured message inquiries per day.

  • Customers faced 2-3 hour wait times for order confirmation
  • Frequent manual calculation errors in delivery distance fees
  • Zero consolidated record of daily customer delivery commitments
The Engineering Solution

Dedicated React Web App with Automated Order Routing

Vyomantra engineered a dedicated responsive web application with custom product configurators, integrated payment collection, and direct WhatsApp webhook routing.

  • Sub-second page speed on 4G mobile connections
  • Instant payment confirmation via Razorpay UPI & cards
  • Automated WhatsApp dispatch slip sent straight to baking staff
  • Admin inventory toggle to mark daily flavors sold out
