The Operational Problem

Disconnected Spreadsheets & Overlooked Sales Inquiries

The client's commercial operations team managed high-ticket customer inquiries across disconnected Excel spreadsheets, personal WhatsApp numbers, and email threads. Deals frequently stalled because reps forgot follow-ups after initial price quotes were sent.

  • Zero visibility into deal stage progression or sales rep velocity
  • Up to 20% lead leakage due to missing reminder mechanisms
  • End-of-month reporting required 10+ hours of manual data collation
The Technical Implementation

Unified Real-Time CRM with Intelligent Follow-Up Triggers

Vyomantra engineered a bespoke cloud CRM architecture with real-time websocket synchronization, automated webhook ingestion from web landing pages, and automated WhatsApp/email alert queues.

  • Immediate lead assignment based on territory and agent availability
  • Automated follow-up dispatching reducing churn by 35%
  • Role-based data security protecting confidential customer catalogs
  • Instant executive PDF reports delivered automatically every Monday
